# Consent message for athletes (template)

Translate to the athlete's language and adapt the tone. The coach sends it; keep
the athlete's answer.

---

Hi <athlete name>,

to plan and review your training I use an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic)
connected to your intervals.icu account. It reads the data you share with me on
intervals.icu — workouts, heart rate, pace and power, and wellness data such as
sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, weight and how you feel — and uses it to draft
your weekly plan.

- I review and approve every workout before you see it: the assistant never
  changes your calendar on its own.
- Your data is used only for your coaching. It is kept in my private coaching
  folder on my computer and processed by the AI service to prepare the plan.
- You can ask me at any time to stop using the assistant for your coaching, or
  to delete the notes I keep about you.

Are you OK with this? A simple "yes" in reply is enough.

<coach name>
